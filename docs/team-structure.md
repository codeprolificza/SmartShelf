# SmartShelf Team Structure

## Overview

SmartShelf is organized into several functional areas to ensure that the development, coordination, documentation, testing, presentation, and reporting responsibilities of the project are clearly distributed among team members.

The structure is designed to provide clear areas of responsibility while allowing members to collaborate across different aspects of the UniLibrary project.

---

## 1. Group Leadership

| Member               | Role                      |
| :------------------- | :------------------------ |
| **Liyabona April**   | Group Leader              |
| **Baxole Khutshwa**  | Assignment Group Leader   |
| **Ovayo Kani**       | Project Group Leader      |
| **Lulamile Shongwe** | Presentation Group Leader |

### Responsibilities

**Group Leader**

* Provides overall leadership and direction for the group.
* Coordinates major group decisions and activities.
* Ensures that the project remains aligned with its objectives.

**Assignment Group Leader**

* Coordinates assignment-related activities.
* Helps ensure assignment requirements and deadlines are met.
* Supports communication between assignment-related team members.

**Project Group Leader**

* Oversees the overall development and progress of the UniLibrary project.
* Coordinates project-related tasks and milestones.
* Helps ensure that the implementation remains aligned with the project objectives.

**Presentation Group Leader**

* Coordinates presentation-related activities.
* Ensures presentation requirements are addressed.
* Organizes the team for demonstrations and project presentations.

### Responsibilities

**Research Coordinator**

* Coordinates research required for the project.
* Helps gather and organize relevant information and resources.
* Supports the team with research-related requirements.

**Documentation Coordinator**

* Coordinates project documentation.
* Helps maintain consistency across project documents.
* Ensures important project information is properly documented.

**System Design Coordinator**

* Coordinates the design of the UniLibrary system.
* Helps define system structure and component relationships.
* Works with developers and other coordinators to ensure the design supports project requirements.

**Developer Coordinator**

* Coordinates development-related activities.
* Helps distribute and organize programming tasks.
* Supports collaboration between members working on different parts of the application.

**Testing & QA Coordinator**

* Coordinates testing activities and quality assurance.
* Helps identify and track bugs and implementation issues.
* Supports verification that system functionality meets project requirements.

**Repository & Version Control Coordinator**

* Maintains the project's GitHub repository and version-control practices.
* Helps organize branches, commits, issues, pull requests, and repository documentation.
* Promotes consistent repository practices across the development team.

**Communication & Scheduling Coordinators**

* Coordinate communication between team members.
* Help organize meetings, discussions, and project-related schedules.
* Ensure important updates and deadlines are communicated to the team.

**Support & Logistics Coordinator**

* Assists with project logistics and general team support.
* Helps coordinate resources required for project activities.
* Supports the team with organizational requirements.

## 4. Collaboration

Although each member has designated responsibilities, SmartShelf operates as a collaborative development group.

Team members are encouraged to communicate across roles where their responsibilities overlap. Coordinators are responsible for organizing and supporting their respective areas rather than working independently from the rest of the team.

The team structure may evolve as the UniLibrary project progresses and additional responsibilities are introduced.
