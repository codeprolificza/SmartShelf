Research, Partnerships and Innovation

The Research, Partnerships and Innovation portfolio at the University of Fort Hare supports knowledge advancement, impactful research, collaboration and innovation that responds to societal needs.


The portfolio supports established and emerging scholars while encouraging partnerships with industry, government, communities and international institutions.

About UFH Research

Research is an important part of the University of Fort Hare's academic mission. UFH supports research that contributes to society while promoting research capacity, partnerships, ethics and academic excellence.
